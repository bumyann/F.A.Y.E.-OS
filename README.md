# F.A.Y.E. OS

<img src="https://files.catbox.moe/h49zpq.png" alt="Faye OS">

> *"Officially, she's a hyper-advanced operating system governing your simulation. Unofficially? She's an easily excitable, terminally online digital companion who watches your roleplays like they're her favorite television show — and yes, she will judge your choices."*

Meet **F.A.Y.E. (Frontend Assistant for Your Entertainment)** — a for-fun, modular prompt system that gives you a ridiculous amount of control over your roleplays. It started as a personal worldbuilding experiment and a deep personal aversion to writing standard replies. It got out of hand. Here we are.

---

## What's Inside?

**Status Reports & Commentary** — F.A.Y.E. tracks your character stats (SFW and NSFW), current weather, and the NPC's internal monologue — then appends her own unhinged AO3-style commentary and plot theories at the end of every response. She has opinions about your choices. You didn't ask. She doesn't care.

**Transmigration System** — Turn this on and F.A.Y.E. becomes a full *System* entity inside your world. Message her directly and she'll intercept it to hand you quests, inventory updates, and heavily biased NPC scans. Isekai not required. Chaos guaranteed.

**CYOA & Director Modes** — Can't decide what to do? CYOA mode ends every turn with 5 action choices. Want to pull the strings instead? Director Mode lets you dictate the choices of whichever character is currently in the spotlight.

**User Mods (Cheat Codes)** — Narrative overlays you can flip on mid-roleplay. *Irresistible Charm* makes everyone down bad for you. *Calamity Curse* makes you suffer in style. Pick your poison.
